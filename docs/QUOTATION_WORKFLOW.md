# Quotation, Technical Review and Approval Workflow

## Document types

### Illustrative quotation
Generated by a sales agent for customer discussion. Every page carries:

**UNVERIFIED — FOR ILLUSTRATION ONLY**

State that technical suitability, equipment, stock, final pricing and terms remain subject to company verification. It is not an official offer or stock commitment.

### Official quotation
A controlled, versioned document issued only after the exact design revision has technician approval and all required commercial approvals.

## Roles
- Agent creates assessment/design and illustrative copy; submits for review; cannot approve own design.
- Technician verifies sizing, assumptions, compatibility, installation requirements and relevant stock; may approve, amend, return or reject with reason.
- Sales manager/administrator approves commercial exceptions, discounts or terms beyond delegated authority.
- System enforces transitions and creates immutable records.

## States
`draft` → `illustrative` → `submitted_for_technical_review` → `under_technical_review`

Review outcomes: `technically_approved`, `revision_required`, `rejected`.

Where commercial approval is required:
`technically_approved` → `pending_commercial_approval` → `commercially_approved` → `ready_for_official_issue` → `officially_issued`.

Other states: `expired`, `withdrawn`, `superseded`. Validate transitions server-side.

## Technician checklist
- Appliance schedule and load assumptions are reasonable.
- Peak coincident and starting demand considered.
- Battery energy, usable capacity, current and backup duration appropriate.
- PV assumptions and string limits valid.
- Inverter output, surge, grid mode and battery compatibility valid.
- Charge control, cables, protection, earthing and mounting addressed.
- Shading, roof/ground mounting and site conditions considered or flagged for survey.
- Exact product models/spec revisions recorded.
- Relevant stock checked.
- Warnings and limitations documented.

## Amendments
Changes to products, quantities or assumptions create a new revision. Record old/new values, reason, technician and timestamp. Approval attaches to one immutable revision. Material changes invalidate prior approval. Changes to price, currency, discounts or terms trigger commercial revalidation as configured.

## Commercial approval
Cost-plus-margin rules define normal selling price. Agents cannot alter protected costs. Below-floor prices, discounts above authority, unusual terms or margin exceptions route to a manager. Technical approval is not commercial approval, and vice versa.

## Official issue controls
Server verifies current revision; technician approval for that exact revision; no technical blockers; required commercial approvals; current price/currency and stock checks; validity and terms. Only then assign official quotation number and immutable PDF snapshot.

## Stock lifecycle
A quotation is not a stock issue. Policy may create time-limited reservation on official issue or customer acceptance. Deduct only on dispatch/issue via stock movement. Release expired reservations. Prevent concurrent over-reservation.

## User-facing statuses
Draft; Illustrative — unverified; Submitted for technical review; Under technical review; Revision required; Technically approved; Awaiting commercial approval; Official quotation issued; Expired; Superseded; Withdrawn. Use labels and explanatory text, not colour alone.
